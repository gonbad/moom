# /outline

Read the project constitution and current status. Use the following references from `docs/references/`: `workflow.md`, `quality.md`.

Read `docs/living/idea.md`, source notes and resolved input. Write
`docs/living/outline.md` with the opening situation, progression, concrete
payoff and closing action, assigning approximate duration and visual purpose to each
beat. Explain why any character or motion is needed; a diagram or deliberate still
may communicate better than constant movement. Note asset complexity and reuse.

Keep the core story feasible for the chosen duration and workshop/time budget. Do not
start polished drawing or rendering. Resolve non-obvious narrative choices through the
current numbered review, including assumptions and comments on the living outline.
Proceed to `/story` when the outline questions are resolved.
