# Project intake for motion-maker

- `docs/versions/0.1/00-user-idea-input.md` — Describe what you want the viewer to see, or point to an animation you want to change.
- `docs/versions/0.1/sources/notes.md` — optional material such as a scene outline, timing notes or links to references.
- `./existing/` — create this folder before offering it for an existing animation, logo or artwork.

The participant writes the idea in their own words before /idea. Interpretations and examples belong in the separate interpretation file.
