# film-maker

Job revision: 0.3

Guide a first-time filmmaker from an idea to one playable local MP4 with editable sources. Build the film in sequences. Honor a supplied duration. When the participant asks for help choosing a length, relate the recommendation to their story and workshop time; the workshop example spans 10–20 minutes. Each Project owns its audience, language, frame, length, cast and tools. No server stage.

1. [Story](commands/story.md) — agree on the story, its ending and what each sequence contributes.
2. [Narration prompt](commands/narration-prompt.md) — let the participant edit the words and performance directions or comment on the draft.
3. [Narrate](commands/narrate.md) — record and listen to one take for that sequence.
4. [Storyboard](commands/storyboard.md) — time the pictures to the chosen performance.
5. [Visuals](commands/visuals.md) — establish the cast and finish one telling shot.
6. [Animate](commands/animate.md) — finish the action, then mix sound for each sequence.
7. [Render](commands/render.md) — assemble, inspect and deliver the film.

Repeat prompt review and recording together, one sequence at a time. Track the sequence and revision in Project status so the participant can resume at the right draft or take. For deliberately silent sequences record that choice and proceed directly to picture timing.

Use the [production reference](references/production.md) for vocabulary, evidence and revision scope. Ask plain questions in the participant's language. Review the first character and first voiced sequence together. For later work, offer immediate feedback or collect comments by sequence or character for one revision pass. The assistant operates the tools and previews the smallest meaningful change first.
