# film-maker

Job revision: 0.8

Guide a first-time filmmaker from an idea to one playable local MP4 with editable sources. Build the film in sequences. Honor a supplied duration. When the participant asks for help choosing a length, relate the recommendation to their story and workshop time; the workshop example spans 10–20 minutes. Each Project owns its audience, language, frame, length, cast and tools. No server stage.

1. [Idea](commands/idea.md) — edit the whole story in prose, confirm who speaks and finalize the Project slug.
2. [Story](commands/story.md) — adapt the accepted prose into scenes with camera positions and a shot ID for each speaking turn.
3. [Narration prompt](commands/narration-prompt.md) — review one document per sequence, with copyable style and speech for each performance and a folder for the takes.
4. [Narrate](commands/narrate.md) — record separate voices by uninterrupted speaking turn, actors performing together by scene, and continuous solo narration by sequence.
5. [Storyboard](commands/storyboard.md) — time the pictures to the chosen performance.
6. [Visuals](commands/visuals.md) — establish the cast and finish one telling shot.
7. [Animate](commands/animate.md) — finish the action, then mix sound for each sequence.
8. [Render](commands/render.md) — assemble, inspect and deliver the film.

Repeat prompt review and recording together, one sequence at a time. Track the sequence and revision in Project status so the participant can resume at the right draft or take. For deliberately silent sequences record that choice and proceed directly to picture timing.

Use the [production reference](references/production.md) for vocabulary, evidence and revision scope. Ask plain questions in the participant's language. Review the first character and first voiced sequence together. For later work, offer immediate feedback or collect comments by sequence or character for a revision pass, then continue review until the shared closure conditions hold. The assistant operates the tools and previews the smallest meaningful change first.

Use the [Project intake guide](bootstrap.md) to prepare a new Project and explain where to put its idea and materials.
