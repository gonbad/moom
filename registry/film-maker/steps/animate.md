# /animate

Read the project constitution and current status. Use the following references from `docs/references/`: `production.md`.

Confirm final narration takes and measured lengths. Develop the approved look across the board. Select named states per shot, time gestures and sound cues to speech, allow intentional quiet, and keep voice audible over ambience. Render each shot from repeatable local time. Render and preview each sequence. At a continuous-action join, compare the outgoing and incoming camera framing, character poses, positions and direction. Play through the join at speed and carry the ending state forward wherever the action continues in the same view. Once the action works, mix the voice, effects and optional music on separate tracks. Time effects to visible actions and listen to the quietest line with the mix playing. Save selected sources, cues and batch identities in docs/living/production.md. When feedback arrives show the immediate local change first, obtain creative feedback when needed, then propagate. Preserve valid renders.
