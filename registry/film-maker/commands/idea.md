# /idea

Read AGENTS.md, the four documents in docs/constitution/, the installed profile, Job and status in docs/kandoo/living/, and its references/production.md. Resolve the selected Project, open version, current input and submitted reviews. Work under projects/ID/ for media and docs/projects/ID/ for living notes and versioned Markdown reviews.

Follow the workflow constitution’s first Project input procedure and the installed bootstrap when available. Read the selected Project input, the source inventory, supplied material and prior explicit answers. If only template text exists, invite a free-form idea and wait. Preserve the participant’s audience, running time and ending. Help develop missing story beats as proposals. Write the complete story from opening through ending as continuous, editable prose in living/idea.md. Leave scene divisions, camera directions and shot IDs for /story.

During this step, finalize the Project slug from the reviewed title. Apply a provisional-to-final rename using the workflow constitution’s Project naming procedure before handing off to /story. Keep the numeric ID and verify the current input and review links at the new paths.

With the participant, decide who tells the story. Record whether one narrator speaks throughout, characters speak their own dialogue, the film combines both, or it is deliberately silent. For narration identify who the audience hears, their perspective and spoken language. For dialogue identify the speaking characters and language. Distinguish recorded voices heard within the story from the film narrator. Capture unresolved assumptions in the numbered review.

Invite direct edits or inline comments on the prose and answers or overrides in the review. Reconcile both under the shared review constitution. Record explicit or soft confirmation of the resulting prose revision and voice plan before handing off to /story.

Record evidence, pending review and next action in Project and shared status. Apply the four-domain check and show the localized pipeline. Keep human acceptance separate from technical checks.
