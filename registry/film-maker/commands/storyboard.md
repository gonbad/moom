# /storyboard

Read AGENTS.md, the four documents in docs/constitution/, the installed profile, Job and status in docs/kandoo/living/, and its references/production.md. Resolve the selected Project, open version, current input and submitted reviews. Work under projects/ID/ for media and docs/projects/ID/ for living notes and versioned Markdown reviews.

Break the script into sequences, scenes and numbered shots even for a tiny film. For each voiced sequence, use its chosen recording and measured length. Mark phrases and pauses that motivate action, then time the rough HTML pictures to them. Save framing, action, cue offsets and deliberate opening or closing holds in living/board.md. Sketch thumbnails with placeholder silhouettes and assemble a playable animatic. Check the ending and shot boundaries both with and without sound. Ask where the viewer felt rushed or missed a reveal. Adjust order and holds here. Save the reviewed rough cut and identify the hardest representative shot.

Record evidence, pending review and next action in Project and shared status. Apply the four-domain check and show the localized pipeline. Keep human acceptance separate from technical checks.
