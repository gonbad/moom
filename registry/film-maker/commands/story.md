# /story

Read AGENTS.md, the four documents in docs/constitution/, the installed profile, Job and status in docs/kandoo/living/, and its references/production.md. Resolve the selected Project, open version, current input and submitted reviews. Work under projects/ID/ for media and docs/projects/ID/ for living notes and versioned Markdown reviews.

Start from the accepted living/idea.md and its confirmed voice plan. If either is pending, resume /idea with the existing draft and answers. Adapt the reviewed story into living/script.md. Give each sequence its dramatic goal and each scene its setting, visible action and a brief camera note locating the viewer relative to the subject. Describe a change of angle as another shot in the same scene when time and place continue.

In dialogue or mixed films, give each uninterrupted speaking turn a stable dialogue shot ID, speaker, exact spoken text and intended framing. Cover off-screen lines and playback too. Keep consecutive questions in one turn when they form one response. A cut to a listener’s reaction can share the ongoing turn. Label narrator passages separately and identify the shots they cover. Keep translated subtitles separate from spoken text. For single-narrator films, group the narrator’s spoken text by sequence. Mark silent beats. Preserve IDs across edits and reordering.

Present the script for a read-through. Reconcile edits, inline comments and review answers before recording explicit or soft confirmation of the revision and preparing its recording prompts.

Record evidence, pending review and next action in Project and shared status. Apply the four-domain check and show the localized pipeline. Keep human acceptance separate from technical checks.
