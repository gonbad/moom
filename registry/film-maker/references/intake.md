# Project intake for film-maker

- `docs/versions/0.1/00-user-idea-input.md` — Tell the story in your own words. A voice transcription or unfinished scene is enough to begin.
- `docs/versions/0.1/sources/notes.md` — optional material such as a draft script, notes about the characters or a link to a reference film.
- `./existing/` — create this folder before offering it for a clip, drawing, character reference or audio take.

The participant writes the idea in their own words before /idea. Interpretations and examples belong in the separate interpretation file.
