# /brief

Read the project constitution and current status. Use the following references from `docs/references/`: `workflow.md`, `quality.md`.

Gate: `docs/living/idea.md` exists and has a closed review. Author `docs/living/brief.md`:
1. **Target Reader & Tone:** Primary audience, starting context, language/script needs,
   and analytical or practical tone.
2. **Agreed Reader Outcome & Thesis:** What the reader will understand or do afterward,
   and the central claim this piece defends from its verified sources.
3. **Key Arguments & Differentiation:** Supporting pillars, counterexamples or limits
   acknowledged, and how this piece stays faithful to the actor/action boundary in `idea.md`.
4. **Deliverable Constraints & Acceptance Expectations:** Target length, format, code
   listings, diagrams or tables, web/mobile reading requirements and any explicit
   delivery overrides. The page must explain itself without a speaker.

Put consequential unresolved angles or tradeoffs in
`docs/versions/VERSION/NN-questions.md` for review in Obsidian. Once review closes, record the confirmed choices
without adding an extra approval round, and hand off to `/story`.
