# /content

Read the project constitution and current status. Use the following references from `docs/references/`: `workflow.md`, `quality.md`.

Gate: `docs/living/story.md` exists and has a closed review. Draft the complete narrative in `docs/living/content.md` by
implementing the exact section order, actor/action sequence and evidence map in
`story.md` (reflecting any owner edits to `story.md`). Build and maintain a
claim-to-source map in `docs/living/content.md` or a companion `.md` note
under `docs/versions/VERSION/`; check primary sources for volatile facts and
mark inference explicitly. Keep illustrative examples distinct from observed results,
and never include credentials or private account details.

Present manageable sections if needed, then invite a complete reading of
`docs/living/content.md` in Obsidian. Reconcile omissions and cross-section
contradictions against `idea.md`, `brief.md` and `story.md`. Read the page as an
independent reader: add missing context or explanations that would otherwise require
a speaker. Keep editorial audit tables out of public prose unless requested. A paper, handbook or
presentation may adapt structure in the Job rather than flattening into generic prose.
Record editorial review status separately from technical readiness and route to
`/layout`.
