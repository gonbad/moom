# Blog quality criteria

- **Source Fidelity:** The published post must faithfully represent the source material and user requirements.
- **Visual Autonomy:** Each post selects the layout best suited to its topic without forcing a rigid sitewide template.
- **Offline Self-Containment:** The post and its offline ZIP archive must operate cleanly without network access.
- **Zero-Remote Leaks:** No external CDN resources (`http://` or `https://` in `<script>` or `<link>`).
- **Human Acceptance:** Passing automated builds or linters does not replace human editorial review.
