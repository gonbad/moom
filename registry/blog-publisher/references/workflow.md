# An authored publication, one Project per post

A post may be an article, handbook, paper, presentation or interactive explanation.
Ask what the reader should understand or do, the evidence available, language, desired
format and delivery route. Preserve the creator's actual actor/action sequence. An
article about what the reader does must not quietly become instructions to an AI.

`job-idea` produces the brief and identifies public/private source boundaries.
`job-content` builds the narrative, claim/source map and concrete examples.
`job-layout` chooses a layout appropriate to this post, with realistic content.
`job-build` produces web output and a verified standalone offline package.
`job-deploy` delivers locally or publishes to the already authorized concrete target.
Fidelity, editorial acceptance, browser verification and deployment are separate facts.

Keep content, layout decisions, source contract and current state in the Project's
living docs. Preserve raw requests and numbered review rounds in its open version.
Review manageable sections followed by a complete reading: good rendering does not
prove a faithful explanation. New posts start private/draft; creation must not require
a machine state manifest, an upstream template or a parent repository.

A shared authored index is its own Project. Change it deliberately when a post is
ready for discovery; newest-first ordering and summaries are editorial choices.
A new Job revision never republishes older Projects implicitly. For an iteration,
record the changed reader outcome, affected content/layout and publication decision;
retain the prior delivered revision until replacement is explicitly selected.
