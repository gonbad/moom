# Project intake for blog-publisher

- `docs/versions/0.1/00-user-idea-input.md` — Tell us what you want to publish, or point to the draft you already have.
- `docs/versions/0.1/sources/notes.md` — optional material such as draft text, interview notes or source links.
- `./existing/` — create this folder before offering it for an existing publication bundle, photographs or illustrations.

The participant writes the idea in their own words before /idea. Interpretations and examples belong in the separate interpretation file.
