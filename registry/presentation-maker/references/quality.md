# What to check

- The requested problem, audience action, sequence, language and required examples
  survive the transformation; attractive added material does not replace them.
- Each chunk shows the source relationship and the new decisions a person can assess.
- The full deck has consistent terminology, complete coverage and no contradictions
  between individually reviewed chunks.
- The HTML works offline, text is escaped, navigation works, and content remains
  readable without a mandatory network resource.
- Receipts describe actual checks. A passing render is not content approval.
