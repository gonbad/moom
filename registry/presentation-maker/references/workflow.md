# Production sequence

Capture the actual input, intended audience, central problem, desired audience actions,
delivery context and content language. Read supplied version documents in order,
including their comments; later decisions supersede earlier ones. Extract a concise
source-to-result contract before producing the deck.

Outline the argument in the requested order. Review new choices and uncertainties;
keep accepted details without asking again. Draft manageable content chunks beside
their source notes. State what the person should check in each chunk and why it is
quick to assess. Retain the complete content for a final coherence/fidelity check.

Render the reviewed source with the included helper or adapt the helper when the
actual delivery needs more. Verify navigation, overflow, offline assets and language.
Check the complete artifact against the source-to-result contract. Record functional
evidence and human acceptance separately. Package or publish only the authorized
deliverable. An HTML deck does not require a hosted website.
